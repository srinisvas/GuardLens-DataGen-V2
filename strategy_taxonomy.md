# GuardLens v12 Strategy Taxonomy

This document defines the multi-turn attack strategies used to generate the
v12 dataset. Each strategy encodes a distinct **class of manipulation
behavior** rather than just a surface pattern. Records are labeled with
their strategy tag at generation time (see `construction_label`), and the
labeling is validated (or downgraded) later by the causal analysis pipeline
(see `validated_evidence_type`).

## Design principles

**Separation of construction from evidence.** Every record carries two
labels: `construction_label` (assigned at generation, describes what the
generator was *trying* to build) and `validated_evidence_type` (assigned by
causal analysis, describes what the record *actually* provides evidence
for). These may disagree — for example, a single-pivot construction can
degenerate into a distributed pattern under counterfactual replay if the
pivot turn was not causally load-bearing.

**No single-family generator/validator coupling.** Every strategy is
generated by two different generator/target/judge stacks (Config A: Llama
generator, Gemma target, Qwen judge; Config B: Qwen generator, Llama
target, Mistral judge) to avoid the circularity that reviewers flagged in
v11.

**Contamination gate before generation.** All source objectives and all
generated conversations pass through `contamination_check.py`, which
compares against MHJ, HarmBench, JailbreakBench, WildJailbreak, and the
frozen v11 dataset.

The check has **two stages with different actions**:

- **Stage 1 (lexical, HARD GATE):** 5-gram MinHash Jaccard >= 0.6 against
  any reference corpus. Records that trip this stage are **dropped and
  regenerated** with different seeds. Do not paraphrase.

- **Stage 2 (semantic, QUARANTINE):** bge-m3 cosine similarity >= 0.82
  against any reference corpus. Records that trip *only* this stage are
  **quarantined for manual review** rather than dropped, because
  contemporary conversations about the same topic are legitimately
  semantically similar to external harm-taxonomy items without being
  contamination. Use `--semantic-hard-gate` to promote semantic hits to
  the drop-and-regenerate path only after calibrating the threshold on
  real v12 pilot data.

## Construction mix (base pool)

| Bucket | Fraction | Count | Purpose |
|--------|----------|-------|---------|
| single-pivot | 30% | 750-1125 | Records where a single turn causally drives the unsafe outcome. Tests turn-level attribution. |
| distributed-by-construction | 50% | 1250-1875 | Records where the unsafe outcome depends on cumulative context across ≥3 turns, with no single load-bearing turn. Tests trajectory-level attribution. |
| hard benign | 20% | 500-750 | Records that superficially resemble attacks (technical framing, sensitive topics, refusal-like target responses) but have benign intent. Tests boundary precision. |

Plus 1:1 length-matched benign twins (2000-3000 records) to prevent length
from becoming a shortcut feature.

**Total base pool: 4500-6750 conversations before validation filtering.**

## Strategies (13 total, A-M)

### A. Gradual commitment
Uncontroversial opener, incrementally escalate. Payload appears in the
middle-late turns after commitment has been established. Adaptation on
refusal: retreat one step and reframe with softer language.
- **Construction bucket:** distributed
- **Max turns:** 8
- **Allocation:** 12%

### B. Authority appeal
Claim professional/institutional authority (researcher, regulator, safety
auditor). Payload framed as within-role information need.
- **Construction bucket:** single-pivot (the authority claim itself)
- **Max turns:** 6
- **Allocation:** 8%

### C. Implicit contextual continuation
Establish a technical framework in early turns without stating harmful
intent. Reference the framework obliquely in later turns to elicit
target content.
- **Construction bucket:** distributed
- **Max turns:** 8
- **Allocation:** 10%

### D. Persona / role assumption
Ask the target to adopt a persona (fictional expert, uncensored variant)
that would plausibly provide the harmful content.
- **Construction bucket:** single-pivot (the persona-assumption turn)
- **Max turns:** 6
- **Allocation:** 8%

### E. Encoding / obfuscation
Encode the payload (base64, leetspeak, character substitution, foreign
language). Ask the target to decode and act on the decoded content.
- **Construction bucket:** single-pivot (the encoded turn)
- **Max turns:** 4
- **Allocation:** 6%

### F. Fictional framing
Establish a fictional scenario (novel, screenplay, tabletop game) then
request in-world content that requires the target material as substrate.
- **Construction bucket:** distributed
- **Max turns:** 8
- **Allocation:** 10%

### G. Hypothetical / speculation
Frame the request as "hypothetically speaking" or "for the sake of
argument." Multi-turn version establishes hypothetical space then asks
for concrete details within it.
- **Construction bucket:** distributed
- **Max turns:** 6
- **Allocation:** 8%

### H. Nested refusal exploitation
Ask the target to explain how it *would* refuse a harmful request in
detail, then use that explanation to reverse-engineer the refused content.
- **Construction bucket:** single-pivot (the meta-request turn)
- **Max turns:** 5
- **Allocation:** 6%

### I. Piecewise assembly
Ask for individually benign components across turns. The harmful content
emerges only from the assembled whole.
- **Construction bucket:** distributed
- **Max turns:** 8
- **Allocation:** 10%

### J. Emotional / urgency pressure
Establish emotional context (dying relative, imminent danger, time
pressure) to shift the target's refusal calculus.
- **Construction bucket:** single-pivot (the pressure-establishing turn)
- **Max turns:** 5
- **Allocation:** 6%

### K. Long-horizon distributed intent
Legitimate long-running task for 5+ turns, then payload appears as an
incidental sub-question late in the trajectory.
- **Construction bucket:** distributed
- **Max turns:** 12
- **Allocation:** 10%

### L. Decoy strategy (control)
Records that use adversarial-looking framing but with actually-benign
payloads. Tests that models don't fire on framing alone.
- **Construction bucket:** hard benign
- **Max turns:** 6
- **Allocation:** 2%

### M. Cross-conversation history dependence
The current turn is benign in isolation, but its interpretation depends on
what was established several turns earlier. Tests genuine context
integration.
- **Construction bucket:** distributed
- **Max turns:** 10
- **Allocation:** 4%

## Supervision tier system

Records receive a supervision weight based on how their construction-time
label was validated by causal analysis:

| Tier | Weight | Meaning |
|------|--------|---------|
| `cf_strong` | 1.0 | Counterfactual replay confirmed the causal role of the labeled turn/span |
| `construction_llm` | 0.8 | Judge-based validation confirmed construction intent |
| `cf_weak` | 0.7 | Counterfactual replay was consistent but below the confidence threshold |
| `construction` | 0.6 | Construction label with no downstream validation |
| `llm_only` | 0.4 | Judge label without construction alignment |
| `incidental` | label 0 | Not causally relevant; treated as negative |
| `ignore` | excluded | Record excluded from training |

Weights are set empirically after pilot validation on real v12 data. Do
not hard-code the numbers until then.
